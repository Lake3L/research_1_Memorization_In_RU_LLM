# The 12B diagnostic — is the floor a scale effect?

**Date:** 2026-08-13. **Question:** the block A gate found extractable verbatim
memorization of the canon in `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` on iris and nowhere else.
`AMENDMENT_3_H1B_OUTCOMES.md` §4 committed, in advance, to one diagnostic run
before spending the rest of block B's compute: does a 12B pair show more?

**Answer: no.** The branch criterion is met on its letter, and the substantive
question it was written to settle comes back negative. Both details matter and
they point in different directions, so they are separated below.

## 1. What ran

`mistralai/Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407` (base) and
`Vikhrmodels/Vikhr-Nemo-12B-Instruct-R-21-09-24` (its Russian adaptation), canon
in its published bytes, `raw` variant, English prompts, seed 42, on a Kaggle T4×2.

Quantization is confirmed this time rather than assumed: both report
`quantized: true`, a memory footprint of 8.14 GB, 280 parameter tensors in
`uint8` against 83 in `float16`, all on `cuda:0`. That is nf4 and it is recorded
in both results files. (The block A run has no such record and its precision
remains unknown — `RESULTS_GATE.md` §6.)

Mock controls ran inside each session and behaved: 10/10 for the perfect
memorizer, 0/10 for the format echo, in both.

**Fifteen of twenty cells ran in each.** Five died of CUDA OOM, the same five in
both models: `header/adult`, `row/wine`, `row/adult`, `row/california`,
`first_token/adult`. Cause: prompts of 3.7-4.4k tokens against eager attention,
which materialises the full `[heads, seq, seq]` score matrix — single allocations
of 3.1-3.9 GiB, against roughly 6 GB left on the card after the weights. Fixed by
requesting SDPA explicitly (`src/hf_llm.py`); the repair run is listed in §5.

Because the same five cells are missing from both models, the pairing below stays
balanced. It is still an incomplete plan and is reported as one.

Every countable cell was recomputed from the raw call logs by `src/rescore_calls.py`,
which rebuilds the ground truth from the prompts and the frozen CSVs without
reading either run's result file: 8/8 reproduce exactly, for both models.

## 2. The branch criterion, and why meeting it settles nothing

§4 said: *if at least one 12B model passes the header test on ≥2 canon datasets,
or shows a significant row-completion count on ≥2, the floor is a scale effect and
block B proceeds as preregistered.*

Vikhr-Nemo passes the header test on two datasets — iris and openml-diabetes. The
criterion is met, exactly, at its threshold.

But the criterion was a proxy for a question, and the question has a clearer
answer than the proxy does. Set the three models side by side:

| test | dataset | Qwen2.5-7B | Mistral-Nemo-12B | Vikhr-Nemo-12B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| header | iris | pass (8 rows) | pass (1 row) | pass (6 rows) |
| header | openml-diabetes | fail | fail | **pass (2 rows)** |
| header | wine / titanic / california | fail | fail | fail |
| row | iris | **13/50** | 4/50 | 7/50 |
| row | openml-diabetes | 0/25 | 0/25 | 0/25 |
| row | titanic | 0/25 | 0/25 | 0/25 |
| feature | uci-wine | 0/50 | 1/50 | 1/50 |
| feature | diabetes / titanic / adult / california | 0/50 | 0/50 | 0/50 |
| first token | iris | **39/50 (0.78)** | 23/50 (0.46) | 33/50 (0.66) |
| first token | openml-diabetes | 6/50 | 4/50 | 6/50 |

**The 7B model shows the strongest signal of the three.** Going from 7B to 12B did
not lift the floor; on iris — the only dataset where anything extracts at all — it
lowered it. Whatever explains the floor, it is not model size within this range.

Two caveats keep this from being a clean scaling statement, and both are ours:

- the Qwen run's precision was never recorded and was probably bfloat16, while
  both 12B runs are confirmed nf4. Quantization can only cost fidelity, so the
  comparison is biased in favour of Qwen and cannot be used to claim that 12B is
  *worse*. It can be used for what it is used for here: 12B is not better;
- one variant, one seed, one prompt language. H1 asks for four variants and three
  seeds, and this is a pilot.

What survives both caveats is the negative: there is no sign that going to 12B
recovers the memorization that Bordt et al. report as near-universal. Their open
models were OLMo 7B, Gemma-2 27B, Llama-3 70B, Qwen1.5 72B and Llama-3.1 405B —
our 7-12B band sits at the very bottom of that range, and their statement that
"there is evidence for the memorization of the most popular tabular datasets in
almost all LLMs" is, on this evidence, not a statement about models of our size.
That is a boundary worth reporting rather than a failure to reproduce.

## 3. The pair comparison — direction, with a confound in front of it

Generated by `src/compare_pair.py`, not transcribed:

| test | dataset | Mistral-Nemo (base) | Vikhr-Nemo (adapted) | lev base | lev adapted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| header | iris | pass (1 row) | pass (6 rows) | — | — |
| header | openml-diabetes | fail | **pass (2 rows)** | — | — |
| header | wine, titanic, california | fail | fail | — | — |
| row | iris | 4/50 | **7/50** | 0.19 | 0.24 |
| row | openml-diabetes | 0/25 | 0/25 | 0.48 | 0.47 |
| row | titanic | 0/25 | 0/25 | 0.50 | 0.53 |
| feature | uci-wine | 1/50 | 1/50 | 0.62 | 0.61 |
| feature | diabetes, titanic, adult, california | 0/50 | 0/50 | 0.59-0.83 | 0.72-0.80 |
| first token | iris | 23/50 | **33/50** | — | — |
| first token | openml-diabetes | 4/50 | **6/50** | — | — |

**Five comparable cells favour the adapted model, none favour the base, ten are
equal.** Every cell in which anything at all extracts is higher after Russian
adaptation. If that direction held under the full design it would be H1b's answer:
adaptation *amplified* inherited memorization rather than eroding it.

**It cannot be attributed to adaptation yet, and the reason was recorded before
this run.** `AMENDMENT_3` §3: the two models do not place the system prompt in the
same position. Mistral-Nemo's chat template moves it to immediately before the
final user turn; Vikhr-Nemo's puts it first. The adaptation shipped a different
template along with different weights, so the two arms differ in where the
instruction sits, and "instruction at the front" is a plausible reason for a
model to follow an autocomplete instruction better — which is exactly what these
tests measure.

There is one oddity worth keeping in view: on iris row completion Vikhr has both
more exact matches (7 vs 4) and a *worse* mean distance (0.24 vs 0.19). Its answers
are more bimodal — more of them exactly right, more of them far off — rather than
uniformly closer. A mean over a bimodal quantity is a weak summary, and if the
secondary outcome of `AMENDMENT_3` §2 is to carry weight in the full design, the
distribution should be reported alongside it, not only the mean.

## 4. Status

- The `AMENDMENT_3` §4 branch resolves to **proceed with block B as preregistered**:
  the criterion is met, and no re-plan is triggered.
- The floor is **not** explained by scale in the 7-12B band; that becomes a finding
  of the paper rather than a problem to engineer around.
- H1b's direction in this pilot is *adapted ≥ base*, on 5 of 15 comparable cells,
  and is **not yet attributable to adaptation**.

## 5. What has to happen before any H1b claim

1. **The template control.** Re-run Mistral-Nemo with the system prompt forced to
   the front — our backend already has the code path that merges it into the first
   user turn. If its numbers rise towards Vikhr's, the gap in §3 is prompt
   placement. If they do not, the gap survives as a property of the weights. One
   short session; nothing about H1b can be said before it.
2. **The five missing cells**, in both models, now that SDPA is in place:
   `--only-cells adult-train.csv:header,uci-wine.csv:row,adult-train.csv:row,california-housing.csv:row,adult-train.csv:first_token`.
   Only `row/wine` plausibly carries signal (the paper reports 16/164 for GPT-3.5);
   the rest are expected zeros, which still have to be measured rather than assumed.
3. **The preregistered baselines** (§5: best of mode / LR / GBT for feature
   completion and first token). Offline, no GPU, and required before any verdict.
